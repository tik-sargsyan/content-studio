1. Selfie → Pro Photoshoot

Photo Editing

Turn an ordinary selfie into a professional studio/location photoshoot — same person, elevated.

Input prompt (the "before" — user brings their own)
An ordinary amateur phone selfie of a fictional 24-year-old woman with warm medium-brown skin, casual t-shirt, slightly messy home background, flat phone-camera lighting, plain snapshot, not retouched. Fictional person, not a real individual. 3:4.
Transform prompt · NanoBanana (gemini-3-pro-image) i2i · single input→output
Transform this into a PROFESSIONAL EDITORIAL STUDIO PHOTOSHOOT of the SAME person — keep the exact same face, identity, skin tone and hair. Elevate everything else: dramatic soft key light with gentle rim separation, clean seamless studio backdrop, stylish modern wardrobe, magazine-grade natural retouching (keep skin texture), shallow depth of field, confident pose. Premium, believable, not plastic. 3:4.
Model: gemini-3-pro-image · aspect 3:4 · single-scene image→image (NOT multi-scene)

2. Product → Clean Catalog Shot

Products & E-commerce

An amateur phone photo of a generic unbranded skincare serum glass dropper bottle sitting on a cluttered kitchen counter, poor mixed lighting, distracting background, ordinary snapshot. No visible brand names or logos. 1:1.
Transform prompt · NanoBanana (gemini-3-pro-image) i2i · single input→output
Transform this into a CLEAN PROFESSIONAL E-COMMERCE CATALOG SHOT of the SAME product — keep the exact same bottle shape, color and proportions. Pure white seamless background, soft even studio softbox lighting, subtle natural reflection beneath, crisp sharp focus, product centered, gentle shadow, marketplace-ready (Amazon-style). No text, no logos. 1:1.
